# Journal Positioning

## Purpose

Judge journal fit from the completed evidence chain, not topic prestige or data volume.

## Default ladder

- `Immunity / JEM`: mechanism-first immune or tissue-organ logic with a strong causal story; maximal therapeutic completion is not always required.
- `Cancer Cell / Nature Cancer / Cancer Discovery`: broad disease relevance, strong in-vivo causality, and a crisp translational finish.
- `Cancer Research / Cancer Immunology Research / JITC / Nature Communications`: strong biology with a meaningful but not fully closed mechanism or translational package.
- `Cell Reports`: credible biology with a more descriptive or incomplete mechanistic finish.

Treat this ladder as a starting heuristic, not a guarantee. Scope, novelty, field competition, cohort quality, and journal policy can change the fit.

## Required judgment

When assessing journal fit:

1. rank the realistic options;
2. state the evidence-supported ceiling;
3. name the main blocker as `logic`, `causality`, `validation`, or `finish`;
4. identify the single experiment or revision most likely to change that ceiling;
5. distinguish essential work from attractive but non-decisive additions.

Avoid generic encouragement and do not equate more panels with a higher journal tier.
